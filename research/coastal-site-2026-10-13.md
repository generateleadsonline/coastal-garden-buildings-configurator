## 2. Coastal's Lugarde pages

### Headline finding: no prices anywhere
No page on the site shows a price. A search of all 178 pages for "£", "&pound;", "VAT", "price" in Lugarde page text found none. The only pricing references are:
- "showing you all the options and prices" (about the Lugarde configurator) on /services/design-your-own-bespoke-garden-building-with-3d-configurator/
- "Check out our online configurator and get designing your own and the cost will be itemised for you too !" on /summerhouses-log-cabins-christchurch-dorset-new-forest/
- "Start designing your own with our 3D tool, where you can also see the prices" on /hot-tubs/ (Garden Games Room)
- "The Hut is affordably priced" on /garden-office/
- "Installation charges vary according to your chosen building so please get in touch for a specific quotation." on /services/installation/
- Removals: "prices are available upon request." on /services/removals/

So the configurator is the only place a Coastal visitor sees a Lugarde price.

## 8. Conflicts and inconsistencies (to confirm with Coastal)

1. **Install radius: 20 vs 25 miles.** About page "within a 20-mile radius of our show site"; Installation, Site Survey and Hut pages "25 mile radius".
2. **Years of experience.** "since 1979" / "EST.1979" (47 years in 2026) vs homepage "over 40 years of experience". Not a contradiction, but the homepage figure is stale.
3. **Fitter experience.** About and Recent Installs: fitters "some of whom have worked with us for over 20 years"; Installation page: fitters "have over twenty years industry experience between them" (a much weaker claim).
4. **Phone typo.** Finishing Touches page says "01425 27400" (missing a digit); everywhere else 01425 274000 / 01425 274 000.
5. **Opening hours.** Only "Winter Opening times" published (Mon-Sat 9am-4:30pm, Sun 10.30am-4.00pm); no summer hours. Hut brochure says staff help "7 days a week", consistent.
6. **Configurator link attribution.** The configurator page has two links: the in-text "3D configurator" link goes to `https://lugarde-configurator.com/` WITHOUT the dealer token; only the "View configurator" button carries `?dealerToken=40babb820bb07ab118b01a927d23be6f`.
7. **Log thickness.** Coastal's "Standard features" graphic: "Top quality pine available in 28mm, 44mm or 68mm"; C1 Car Port page text: "28mm or 44mm"; Lugarde brochure: 68 mm is Log cabin system only, and the timber is "first-class spruce" (Coastal says "pine").
8. **Floor and paint included?** Home office article: "All installations comes with guttering and downpipes, laminate flooring and a choice of painted finish." Lugarde brochure: interior floor is "(at extra cost)" and "All our summer houses are supplied without colour treatment as standard."
