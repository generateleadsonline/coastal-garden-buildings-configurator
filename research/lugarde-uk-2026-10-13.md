## 1. Brochure and price list

It is delivered after a request form; no direct PDF link exists on the UK site. Not submitted (per brief). No UK price-list PDF is linked anywhere on the UK pages crawled.
- **Construction drawings are public.** 37 PDF construction drawings ("bouwtekening"/"BT") are linked from product pages via `d7rh5s3nxmpy4.cloudfront.net/CMP11086/files/...`. All but one are outlined vector drawings with **no extractable text** (pypdf returns empty pages), so dimensions must be read visually. The 2026 Select Modern drawing (`BT_Lugarde_Select_Modern_SM282020N001_v2026.pdf`) has text: 200 x 200 cm, wall thickness 28 mm, flat roof, "Not to scale. For design review only".
- Every product page carries a structured spec block (`prod_*` fields) that is the best machine-readable source: width, depth, wall height, ridge height, wall thickness, door and window model numbers, floor area, volume. These are captured below for all 76 UK product pages.

- **Premium** (`/premium-system`, labelled "NEW CONSTRUCTION SYSTEM"): "high quality post and beam construction system". "Posts and wall plates are connected using traditional mortise and tenon joints, while purlins and roof brackets feature dovetail connections." "Complete wall frames slide into grooves within the posts and are mechanically secured using steel strips." Claddings:
  - **Triple Rhombus** - "vertical cladding consists of slim timber profiles"; "Douglas fir, larch and spruce timber with a thickness of 28 mm".
  - **Rebated profile** - "slightly wider boards"; "thickness of 18 mm" (orientation not stated; product copy for Milan says tongue-and-groove).
  - **Swedish overlap** - "overlapping timber boards"; "angled profiles create subtle shadow lines"; "thickness of 12-22 mm".
  - Post size is not stated on this page; the earlier report has 14 cm posts from the configurator (`pro-20`).
- **Pro** (`/pro-system`): "laminated posts measuring 14 x 14 cm. The wall sections slide directly into the posts". "available in various wall thicknesses and dimensions in 10 cm increments". So: visible 140 mm square corner/intermediate posts, horizontal wall boards between them.
- **Prima** (`/prima-system`): "patented construction system"; "the wall sections slide directly into the corner posts without the use of visible nails or screws. An aluminium core inside the corner posts provides additional stability". Shapes: "square, rectangular, pentagonal and octagonal". Roofs listed: "flat roofs, apex roofs and pyramid roofs" (but no current UK Prima product uses apex; UK Prima models are flat, pyramid, octagonal). Post size not stated.
- **Log cabin** (`/log-cabin-system`): "solid timber wall sections are stacked horizontally and connected using a traditional windmill corner joint" (i.e. log ends protrude at every corner). Layouts "square, rectangular, L-shaped or T-shaped". Roofs "flat roofs, apex roofs, pent roofs and asymmetrical". Wall thickness "28 mm, 44 mm, 68 mm and even 90 mm"; "68 mm and 90 mm ... double tongue and groove connection". Log board height is not published on the UK pages (needs the brochure or a drawing read).

## 10. Conflicts and gaps

1. **Door on Budapest:** product page `PE45H` vs drawing B81 `PE24H`. Ridge 288.5 cm on the page vs 2910 mm on the drawing.
2. **Prima roofs:** the Prima system page lists "apex roofs", but no UK Prima product or configurator template (earlier report) has apex.
3. **Premium wall thickness:** the spec field says 18/22/28 mm; that is the cladding thickness (rebated 18, Swedish 12-22, Triple Rhombus 28), not an overall double-clad wall thickness. Model the 140 mm post as the structural depth.
4. **Price basis:** product pages show bare "From ... GBP" with no VAT or delivery wording; the T&Cs say prices are ex-VAT and Ex Works; the configurator cart says incl. VAT and mainland UK delivery. Treat web "from" prices as indicative and let Coastal state its own delivery terms.
5. **Dealer copy is NL/BE:** product pages say "Lugarde has a network of dealers across the Netherlands and Belgium" even on the UK site.
6. **Select catalogue size:** "108 products" vs "You've viewed 24 of 100 products"; only the first page is server-rendered.
7. **Not found on the UK site:** brochure PDF (form-gated), price list, log height, Prima post size, roof pitches other than 21/26 degrees, shingle colour names, fascia/trim material and colours, window opening sizes, delivery surcharges. The brochure is described as including "construction systems, timber types and pricing" and is the likely source for these; it needs a manual request (form not submitted).
